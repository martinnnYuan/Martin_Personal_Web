Web GIS · University of Washington

Seattle transit accessibility

A Web GIS comparing public transportation accessibility across Seattle neighborhoods.

  • MapLibre GL JS
  • Turf.js
  • D3.js
  • C3.js

Overview

Integrated transit stops, transit routes, and neighborhood boundaries from King County Open Data and Seattle Open Data to examine spatial disparities in transit access.

Methods

Built the interactive map with MapLibre GL JS and Turf.js, including neighborhood navigation, layer controls, spatial queries, point-in-polygon analysis, and area calculations. D3.js and C3.js supported dynamic charts.

What the index measures

The neighborhood accessibility index uses the density of in-service transit stops. It describes the distribution of transit service locations rather than network-based walking times.
