GeoAI · GEOG 495

Food access in Washington

A proposed GeoAI framework for studying food deserts and spatial inequality in Washington State.

  • GeoAI
  • Large language models
  • Segment Anything Model
  • Spatial inequality

Overview

Explored how GeoAI could support the identification and analysis of food deserts in Washington State.

Approach

Proposed combining large language models, map-making, and the Segment Anything Model (SAM), with a strategy for evaluating and validating the methods.

Critical perspective

Considered the potential of GeoAI for studying spatial inequality and informing policy alongside its ethical and political limitations.
