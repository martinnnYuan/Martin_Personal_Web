Climate & hurricane costs

Exploring climate change and US hurricane disaster costs through data analysis and visualization.

  • R
  • NCEI data
  • Data visualization

Overview

Investigated the relationship between climate change and hurricane-related disaster costs in the United States.

Data & methods

Used data from the US National Centers for Environmental Information (NCEI), constructed a database, and analyzed the data in R.

Communication

Presented the analysis through data visualizations and class presentations.
