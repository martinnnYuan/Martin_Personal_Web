Wildfire & forest change

Examining wildfire and forest-area change in Washington State between 2000 and 2020.

  • QGIS
  • Spatial analysis
  • Choropleth mapping

Overview

Studied the spatial patterns of environmental and ecological change associated with wildfire in Washington State.

Data & methods

Used Washington State Department of Natural Resources data and spatial analysis in QGIS.

Mapping

Created a choropleth map of forest-area change over the 2000–2020 period to show the spatially uneven patterns of change.
